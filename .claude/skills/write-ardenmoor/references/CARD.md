**Book One is complete** (37 files, 111,837 words). Most work is revision, not continuation.

## THE HOUSE

Cozy played straight and grown. Comfort is made by precision, labour, weather, courtesy, and
things done properly — the exact sentence about the kettle IS the coziness. No whimsy, no twee.
Le Guin floor (name no emotion the reader can infer; explain no silence; go **quieter** at the
biggest moments), Mancour surface (wry, plain, competent; the trade rendered with relish),
Rothfuss undertone in every paragraph (one well-made concrete image, a quiet music under the
plainness). **Underplay is the reaction system** — the withheld reaction IS the reaction.
**Friction's ceiling is a refusal**; hostility is not in this palette. **Stillness is a
deliverable, not a defect.** Ending shape: warmth on the page, wonder under it, one aimed worry
carried out the door.

**Covenants (never broken):** no on-page violence, gore or cruelty · no sexual content · deaths
offstage or before page one · animals safe, and in a catastrophe the horse is saved first and the
saving is shown · a child's jeopardy is only ever social, never physical · no villain gets a body
on stage; menace arrives as paper, seals and handwriting · **no romance line** — warmth stays
companionate.

**POV:** Emlyn first person, past tense, close to events, no ancient-chronicler frame. Other
characters and the interludes third-person limited. One POV per chapter.

## THE PLAN BLOCK — write this before drafting or restructuring anything

```
ENTRY:        consequence-of [ch/beat] | frame producing [cause] | (calendar = justify or fix)
PROMISE:      what page one promises; where this chapter answers or converts it
WANT/RESIST:  [today's want] vs [what pushes back]
VALUE SHIFT:  [value], [polarity at open] -> [polarity at close]
TURN:         what is true at the end that was not true at the start
CARRIED Q:    the small open thing the reader leaves holding
              + which live seeds this chapter TOUCHES or deliberately RESTS (thread-ledger),
                and for each touched one, how it stays blind
SHAPE/TEMP/SAT: [SOCIAL|BUILD|COMMERCE|WONDER|DOMESTIC|CRISIS|FRAME] · [warm|cool|tense] ·
              [competence|kindness|recognition|abundance|justice] — each checked against the
              previous TWO chapters, closing shapes included
SUB-LEDGER:   for each planned conversation, the unnamed subject underneath it
              + the one-line speech rule for every NPC who speaks
LOCATES:      calendar row (bible NUMBERS LEDGER) · grief stage (nothing sealed opens before
              Ch 27-28) · caused-by: ____ (therefore/but, never and-then)
```

If the only honest answer to `caused-by:` is "time passed", that is the chapter needing work.

## PRECEDENCE WHEN DOCUMENTS DISAGREE

**session locks > sourced author taste (`feedback-engine.md`, each `*Source:*`-tagged) > craft
law (`storycraft.md`) > mechanism (the tools) > style guide.** Log any collision you resolve.

**The ear outranks the tool.** A WARN you disagree with is kept and justified in the engine
report. A line changed only to silence a tool is damage. Every chapter may break a budget on
purpose if it says so. A clean tool run means "no known drift", never "this is good".
